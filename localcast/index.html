Good questions.

Which TVs does it work on?

Any Android TV or Google TV running Android 8 or newer: Sony, TCL, Philips, Hisense, Xiaomi, Nvidia Shield, Chromecast with Google TV and most set-top boxes. You install LocalCast on the TV and on your phone.

Do I need internet?

Only to download the apps and, on the free version, to load ads on your phone. Once it's set up, your phone and TV talk to each other over Wi-Fi and the show runs without internet.

Can my team use it too?

Yes. Up to four phones can be connected at once, each with the passcode. They all see the same slides, timer and queue.

What if I forget the passcode?

Any phone that is still connected can set a new one from Settings. If none are, you can reset LocalCast from the TV menu by holding OK for five seconds, which clears everything and starts fresh.

What if my phone can't find the TV?

Some routers and guest networks block devices from discovering each other. Choose "Connect by IP" in the app and type the address shown on the TV screen.

What happens when my phone leaves?

The TV keeps playing. Slides, the timer, the queue and the teleprompter all run on the TV itself.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. It's one purchase of $1.49 (or your local price) tied to your Google account. If you switch phones, tap Restore purchase.

Do I buy Pro on the TV or on my phone?

On your phone. The TV app is free to install, and Pro belongs to the phone you buy it on. That phone gets every Pro feature on any TV it controls. A teammate's phone stays on the free plan unless they buy Pro themselves.
