The honest note about the network

Notes, photos, audio, search, signing and export all work without a signal. ProvoLog connects to the internet only in these five cases:

  1. Ads, free version only. Served by Google AdMob, with Google’s consent form where the law requires it. Pro does not load the ads SDK.
  2. Voice model download. The speech model (about 42 MB) is fetched once from huggingface.co the first time you use transcription.
  3. Map tiles, Pro. OpenStreetMap servers see your IP address and the map area, never your note content.
  4. Address lookup. Android’s Google-provided geocoder turns coordinates into an address. Deny location and nothing is sent.
  5. Purchases. Google Play Billing handles Pro. We receive no payment details.

Content Credentials

A way to check that a photo has not changed since it was signed.

With Pro, photos are signed on your phone using the open C2PA standard. If anyone edits the file afterwards, the signature no longer matches.

  1. Sign on the device

    The signing key is held in the Android Keystore and never leaves the phone.

  2. The record travels with the file

    The signed record is stored inside the JPEG and can include the time, GPS position and your phone’s make and model. It does not include your name, an account or serial numbers. If you share a signed photo, the record goes with it.

  3. Verify it anywhere

    Check a photo in the ProvoLog verify screen, at contentcredentials.org/verify, or with the open-source c2patool command line tool.

warningWhat it is not

  • keyPhotos are signed with a self-issued certificate created on your device. It is not issued by a public authority, so other verifiers will show it as untrusted.
  • timelineThe signing date comes from the phone’s own clock, not a trusted timestamp service.
  • descriptionIt is an integrity aid, not legal certification. It shows a file is unchanged since this app signed it, not that the scene was genuine.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Does it need an account?expand_more

No. There is no sign-up and no email address to give. Install it and start writing.

Where is my data?expand_more

Only on your phone. The database is encrypted with SQLCipher (AES-256), photos, audio and video with AES-256-GCM, and the keys are held in the Android Keystore. App backups are off and we run no servers, so we hold no copy and cannot recover deleted data.

What does the network get used for?expand_more

Five things only: ads on the free tier, the one-time voice model download, Pro map tiles, street-address lookup through Android’s geocoder, and Google Play purchases. Everything else works without a connection. See the details above.

Can I verify a photo?expand_more

Yes, if it was signed with Pro. Use the verify screen in the app, upload the file to contentcredentials.org/verify, or run c2patool. Because the certificate is self-issued, other tools will report it as untrusted, and it is not legal certification.

What happens if I forget my PIN?expand_more

You can unlock with your fingerprint, face or device lock if you enabled them. Because nothing is stored in the cloud, we hold no copy of your notes and cannot recover them for you, so keep an exported PDF or ZIP of anything critical.

What is the duress PIN?expand_more

An optional second PIN. Entering it erases all ProvoLog data on the device immediately and permanently. It exists for situations where you may be forced to unlock the app. There is no undo, so set it up deliberately.

Does it work offline?expand_more

Yes. Notes, photos, audio, search, signing and export work without a signal. The one-time voice model download needs a connection, and the Pro map needs one to load tiles.
